ABOUT DRIVE4U

Ownership made easier. Opportunity made possible.

Drive4U is a South African e-hailing fleet management company helping vehicle owners unlock the earning potential of their cars without taking on every operational responsibility themselves.

We maintain a database of prospective drivers, provide additional training, allocate suitable drivers to vehicles and support day-to-day fleet operations.

Our aim is to make vehicle ownership more convenient while creating practical opportunities for responsible drivers.

Our mission

To make e-hailing investment more accessible, convenient and manageable by connecting vehicle owners with trained drivers and providing dependable fleet management support.

Our vision

To become a trusted e-hailing fleet management partner, empowering vehicle owners to grow their investments while creating sustainable driving opportunities across South Africa.
