FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your questions, answered.

Do I need to own a vehicle to partner with Drive4U?

You should own or be acquiring a suitable vehicle that meets the agreed requirements for e-hailing use.

What does my vehicle need?

Appropriate insurance and a tracking device are required. Drive4U also installs its own dashcam in participating vehicles.

Can I partner while living abroad?

Yes. Drive4U is designed to support South Africans living overseas who need local fleet management assistance.

When will I receive payments?

Payment schedules may be weekly or monthly, subject to your signed agreement and its conditions.

Can I use my vehicle when visiting South Africa?

Requests may be considered subject to advance arrangements, vehicle availability and agreed logistics.
