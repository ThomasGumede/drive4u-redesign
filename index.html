WELCOME TO DRIVE4U

Make your vehicle work for you. Not the other way around.

Owning a car that could generate income is one thing. Managing drivers, vehicle use and daily operations is another.

Drive4U bridges the gap between ownership and e-hailing management with structured driver placement, additional training and dependable operational support.

  • Built for busy professionals and entrepreneurs
  • Suitable for South Africans living abroad

WHAT WE DO

One partner. Multiple ways we help.

Practical support for vehicle owners and the drivers who keep e-hailing moving.

Fleet Management

Day-to-day support for vehicle and driver operations under an agreed management arrangement.

Driver Placement

We connect suitable vehicle owners with screened drivers from our driver community.

Driver Training

Additional training focused on safety, professionalism and responsible vehicle care.

Vehicle Monitoring
